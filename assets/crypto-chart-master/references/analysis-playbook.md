# Analysis playbook

## Fast decision ladder

1. Trend alignment: 15m, 1h, 4h, 1D.
2. Price location: premium, discount, range midline, support, resistance.
3. Liquidity: nearest stops above and below.
4. Displacement: candle body strength and volume.
5. POI: OB/FVG/BPR/support/resistance.
6. Confirmation: BOS/ChoCH/retest/rejection.
7. Risk: stop distance, RR, invalidation.
8. Decision: long, short, or wait.

## Strong long setup

- Higher timeframe bullish or reclaiming key support.
- Sell-side liquidity swept.
- Bullish displacement after sweep.
- FVG/OB forms below price.
- Retest holds with lower selling volume.
- RR >= 1.8R.

## Strong short setup

- Higher timeframe bearish or rejecting key resistance.
- Buy-side liquidity swept.
- Bearish displacement after sweep.
- FVG/OB forms above price.
- Retest rejects with lower buying volume.
- RR >= 1.8R.

## No-trade conditions

- Price trapped in middle of range.
- Conflicting 15m and 4h structure.
- No clear invalidation.
- RR below 1.5R.
- News spike or abnormal spread.
- Low-volume chop.
